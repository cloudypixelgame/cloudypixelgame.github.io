Privacy Policy

Snake Path: Number Puzzle · Last updated 8 October 2026

Snake Path saves your game progress so you don't lose it, shows ads to keep the game free, and sells optional in-game items through Google Play. We never see your payment details, we don't sell your personal information, and you can delete your data at any time.

Who we are

Snake Path: Number Puzzle (package com.cloudypixel.snakepath) is made by Cloudy Pixel Game, an independent developer based in India ("we", "us"). You can reach us at cloudypixelgame@gmail.com.

We do not ask for or collect your email address, contacts, photos, location or payment card details. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play.

In the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland, game analytics and personalised ads are only used if you agree. Until the game can ask you, both stay off there and only non-personalised ads are shown.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your phone (for example, that a new Daily Challenge or your free gift is ready). They are created on your phone and no information is sent to us to do this. You can turn them off in the game (Profile → Notifications) or in your phone's settings.

Advertising

Snake Path is free and shows ads. Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay, which works with advertising networks such as Unity Ads, Google AdMob, AppLovin, Meta Audience Network, Mintegral, Liftoff, Pangle, Moloco, InMobi, Chartboost, DT Exchange and BIGO Ads. These companies may collect the information listed above under their own privacy policies.

  • If you are in a region where consent is required (such as the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland), only non-personalised ads are shown unless you agree. You can change your choice later in the game (Profile → Privacy policy).
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads at any time in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your phone).
  • Buying "Remove Ads" turns off banner and between-level ads. Optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch (for extra coins or hints) stay available.

Third-party services

These services receive information only as described above:

  • Unity Technologies, including Unity Cloud Save, Unity Authentication, Unity Analytics, Unity Cloud Diagnostics, Unity Ads and Unity LevelPlay – Game Player and App User Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services, Google Play Games and Google Play Billing – privacy policy
  • Google AdMob – how Google uses information in ads

How long we keep it

Your saved progress and player ID are kept for as long as you play, and are deleted when you delete them in the game or ask us to. Crash reports and advertising data are kept by the services above for the periods set in their own policies. Uninstalling the game removes the data stored on your phone.

Delete your data

In the game

Open Snake Path, go to Profile and choose Delete my data. This deletes your cloud save, your player account and your game analytics from our servers, and starts the game on your phone from the beginning. It cannot be undone: your level, coins, hints and the items in that save are lost. Purchases such as "Remove Ads" belong to your Google Play account, not to us, so you can get them back with Restore purchases.

By email

  1. Find your Player ID. Open Snake Path, go to Profile and note the Player ID shown at the bottom (press and hold it to copy the full ID).
  2. Uninstall the game (or use Reset progress in the game). Your phone keeps its own copy of your progress; if the game stays installed, it will upload that copy again after we delete it.
  3. Email us at cloudypixelgame@gmail.com with the subject "Snake Path data deletion" and include your Player ID.

We delete your cloud save, player account and game analytics within 30 days and confirm by email.

If the game is already uninstalled

We need your Player ID to find your data. Reinstall Snake Path, sign in with the same Google account you used before, and use Delete my data in the game, or send us the Player ID from the Profile screen. Then uninstall the game again.

If you never signed in with Google, reinstalling may still bring back the same Player ID from your Android backup. If it shows a different ID, email us anyway and we will tell you what we can check.

Android may also keep a copy of the game's settings in your own Google backup. You can remove it in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Backup).

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. Contact us at the address above and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

Snake Path is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security and transfers

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored by Unity and Google on servers that may be located outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union, under their security and data-transfer safeguards.
