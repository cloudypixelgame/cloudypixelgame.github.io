Privacy Policy

Match Number – Number Game · Last updated 19 September 2026

Match Number saves your game progress so you don't lose it, shows ads to keep the game free, and sells optional in-game items through Google Play. We never see your payment details, we don't sell your personal information, and you can ask us to delete your data at any time.

Who we are

Match Number (package com.cloudypixel.matchnumber) is made by Cloudy Pixel Game, an independent developer based in India ("we", "us"). You can reach us at cloudypixelgame@gmail.com.

We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location or payment card details. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play.

In the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland, game analytics and personalised ads are only used if you agree. Until the game can ask you, both stay off there and only non-personalised ads are shown.

Advertising

Match Number is free and shows ads. Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay, which works with advertising networks such as Unity Ads, ironSource, Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, AppLovin, Mintegral and Liftoff. These companies may collect the information listed above under their own privacy policies.

  • If you are in a region where consent is required (such as the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland), only non-personalised ads are shown unless you agree. Where the game asks for your choice, you can change it later in the game.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads at any time in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your phone).
  • Buying "Remove Ads" turns off banner and full-screen ads. Optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch (for free coins or boosters) stay available.

Third-party services

These services receive information only as described above:

  • Unity Technologies, including Unity Cloud Save, Unity Analytics, Unity Ads and Unity LevelPlay – Game Player and App User Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services and Google Play Games – privacy policy
  • Google AdMob – how Google uses information in ads

How long we keep it

Your saved progress and player ID are kept for as long as you play, and are deleted when you ask us to delete them. Crash reports and advertising data are kept by the services above for the periods set in their own policies. Uninstalling the game removes the data stored on your phone.

Delete your data

You can ask us to delete your saved progress and player account from our servers at any time:

  1. Find your Player ID. Open Match Number, go to the Me screen and note the Player ID shown at the bottom (press and hold it to copy the full ID).
  2. Uninstall the game (or use Reset Progress in the game). Your phone keeps its own copy of your progress; if the game stays installed, it will upload that copy again after we delete it.
  3. Email us at cloudypixelgame@gmail.com with the subject "Match Number data deletion" and include your Player ID.

We delete your cloud save, player account and game analytics within 30 days and confirm by email. You can also do this yourself in the game: Me → Privacy → Delete My Data. This cannot be undone: coins, boosters and other items in that save will be lost. A "Remove Ads" purchase is kept by Google Play, not by us, so it is not affected.

If the game is already uninstalled

We need your Player ID to find your data. Reinstall Match Number, sign in with the same Google account you used before, and send us the Player ID from the Me screen. Then uninstall the game again.

If you never signed in with Google, reinstalling may still bring back the same Player ID from your Android backup. If it shows a different ID, email us anyway and we will tell you what we can check.

Android may also keep a copy of the game's settings in your own Google backup. You can remove it in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Backup).

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. Contact us at the address above and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

Match Number is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security and transfers

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored by Unity and Google on servers that may be located outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union, under their security and data-transfer safeguards.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's update notes.
